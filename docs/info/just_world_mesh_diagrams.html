piros2 · session anatomy

How just world_mesh works

One recipe, two machines, seven processes (six with odom:=kp), one RViz window whose closing tears everything down. This is the mesh-first fork session — piros2_world_mesh, aliased just dev and just run — with 6-DoF rgbd_odometry by default and the TSDF as the only fusion accumulator (no cloud_mapper; just world keeps the classic session with the voxel panorama). Since the 2026-08-16 transport rework the camera stream crosses the Wi-Fi exactly once (camera_relay), the depth estimator paces the whole pipeline and feeds the odometry a stamp-identical raw twin, and the point cloud arrives in RViz already posed in the world. The four diagrams below are four different cuts through the same session: what data flows where, what crosses the Wi-Fi link, how the TF tree is owned, and how the recipe starts and stops it all. Reference tables with the measured numbers — every topic, service, and per-node cost — close the page.

Topics, services and measured costs

Every figure below is one of the repo's measured values, not a spec-sheet number. "≈ camera" means the stream is paced by whatever the camera delivers — up to 42–60 fps under the just camera-reset baseline in good light, a measured 11–18 fps in a dim room (auto-exposure trades rate for light).
